Why "Bayan"

A name that means clarity

Bayan means a clear statement, and a financial statement is a bayan mali. Prospect is what lies ahead. We turn your numbers into a clear statement of where the business is today, so you can act on where it goes next.

Bayan /ba·yān/
  1. Statement: a clear, formal account of facts.
  2. Clarity: expression that leaves no ambiguity.
  3. Bayan mali: a financial statement.

About Bayan Prospect

Solid experience behind every engagement

Bayan Prospect is a Dubai-based accounting, tax and fractional CFO practice serving owner-managed businesses in the UAE, Jordan, Oman and Qatar. Our work is led by finance professionals with more than 24 years of hands-on experience across the region, including 18 years at finance-manager level running month-end closes, audits, budgets and reporting for trading, services and free-zone companies.

We bring that depth to every client: UAE Corporate Tax certified expertise, a firm grip of VAT and record-keeping rules, and the judgement to turn accounts into decisions. Clients get the skills of a senior finance team, with every figure reconciled and supported.
